Devolution in South Yorkshire: Education and Employment

South Yorkshire's Combined Authority was legally established in April 2014. The Sheffield City Region Devolution Deal agreed on October 2, 2015, and signed into law on July 27, 2020. Doncaster, Rotherham and Sheffield elected the region's first mayor in 2018 (indicated as a dashed line in the following charts). This dashboard presents 6 time series for the South Yorkshire Combined Authority, focusing on skills, education, and employment.

These charts are based on data from the Office for National Statistics, retrieved using nomis API (Colab Notebook available here).

Source: ONS Annual Population Survey, via the Nomis API. Data downloaded with a loop in this Colab notebook.

Note: Qualification charts end in 2021 as ONS shifted from National Vocational Qualification to the Regulated Qualifications Framework in 2022 (the values are not directly comparable).
